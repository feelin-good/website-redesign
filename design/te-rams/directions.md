# TE × Rams: directions

Brief: a version inspired by Teenage Engineering and Dieter Rams, integrated with Lepton's brand
(logo navy #383070, gear red #d83828). Mocks: `public/directions-te/{1-faceplate,2-field-unit,3-manual}.html`.
Renders: `design/te-rams/renders/`.

| | Concept | Fit | Distinctive | Legibility | Buildable | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · Faceplate (Braun) | 4 | 4 | 2 | 5 | 5 | 20 |
| **2 · Field unit (OP-1)** | 5 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 4 | **22** |
| 3 · The manual (TE guide) | 4 | 4 | 3 | 5 | 5 | 21 |

**Chosen: 2, the field unit.** Lepton builds machines that are controlled from panels: knobs, keys, readouts.
Presenting the company as one well-made instrument is honest (Rams), and playful in a way that still
reads as engineering (TE). The brand colors become the two coloured keys; the red is also the power LED.

- **From 3:** numbered legend lists (●1 ●2 ●3) for specs, giant chapter numerals to open each page.
- **From 1:** Rams restraint on the inner pages (white, quiet, one accent per view) and the
  speaker-grille dot field.
- **Rejected:** 1's rounded-card bento grid (a slop-checklist hit), 3's generic heavy grotesk.
