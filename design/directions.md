**Chosen: A, the drawing set.** Lepton sells drawings before it sells steel: GA drawings, pre-bid
engineering, EPCM. Their buyers (plant heads, EPC procurement, consultants) read drawing sheets
every day, so the format is credible rather than a costume. The title block holds the numbers, the
drawing register holds the 40+ projects, and red revision marks carry the brand red. Nothing in the
category looks like this.

**Borrowed from B:** the material route, mine → receiving → crushing → conveying → stockyard → port,
becomes the Solutions schematic ("Section A–A"). B's specific-fact headline habit is kept for section heads.
**Borrowed from C:** full-bleed photography for the Works (factory) section, and rating-plate data
fields for machine specs.

**Rejected:** C's dark photo hero with an overlay card (a common pattern), and B's ultra-wide type
(reads as a design agency, not an engineering house).
